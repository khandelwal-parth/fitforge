# 🏋️ FitForge Pro — Your Personal AI Fitness Coach

A fully personalized workout and diet plan generator built from scratch.
No subscriptions. No accounts. Just answer a few questions and get your plan.

## 🚀 Live Demo
👉 [Try FitForge Now](https://fitforge-xi.vercel.app)

## 💡 What It Does
Answer 13 questions and FitForge instantly generates:
- A fully personalized **weekly workout plan**
- A tailored **diet and nutrition plan**
- Custom **calorie & macro targets** using the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR formula

## ⚡ Features
- 🎯 Goal-based plans — Muscle Gain or Weight Loss
- 🏠 Bodyweight or Gym Equipment workouts
- 🧠 Smart split optimization based on days available (PPL, Upper/Lower, Full Body)
- 💪 Difficulty adjustment for Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced
- 🥗 Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian meal plans
- 🔄 Shuffle meals for variety
- 📅 7-Day meal rotation
- 💧 Water intake calculator
- 💊 Supplement recommendations
- ⏰ Meal timing based on workout schedule
- 🌙 Dark / Light mode
- 📄 Print your plan

## 🛠️ Built With
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Canva AI (design & prototyping)
